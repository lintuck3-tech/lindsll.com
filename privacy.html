Analytics

Understanding site engagement.

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use the portfolio. Analytics may record information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, referral source, and interactions such as opening a PDF.

The site does not intentionally send names, email addresses, or sensitive case information to Google Analytics. Analytics information is used to understand portfolio engagement and improve the site, not for advertising.

Google may use cookies or similar identifiers to provide Analytics. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services or use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
