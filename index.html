A little about me

It’s hard to sum up who a person is in a paragraph, but the simplest place to start is this: I care deeply. I care about people, the work I do, and whether that work makes someone’s life a little safer or better. I’m practical about how complicated the world can be, but I still believe kindness, curiosity, and thoughtful action can make a real difference. That belief has shaped a career focused on protecting and supporting others, especially young people. My goal is to continue standing up for others and do what I can to protect them.
